01

Profile

Almost all of my time usually goes to the defensive side of security, focusing on the development of intricate security systems or devising new methods to secure pre-existing files. I am usually working on zero-trust security as for me, it is a more interesting challenge to take on and a much more rewarding process. I also use my knowledge of systems engineering, among other things to make these systems work flawlessly.

My path in cybersecurity actually started in 2021 with the log4j vulnerability (CVE-2021-44228). I still remember it as clear as day and I got so hooked on the documentation of this vulnerability that I, as I do with my other interests, went down a huge cybeesecurity rabbithole and found my calling for defensive zero-trust cybersecurity.

My philosophy on doing my projects is always the flow of the project. I always want my projects to be maintainable, the documentation to be readable and the directories to be structured as I believe that good systems are always modular.

If it ain't broke, dont fix it
